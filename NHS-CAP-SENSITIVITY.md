# Income-cap sensitivity: Compton (EXPLORATORY)

**Exploratory.** The Compton data was seen before `ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` existed or before a plan was committed for this run, so nothing here is confirmatory. 
The rate range is sourced (PMMS via FRED). Ranges for tax, insurance, savings and closing costs are placeholders until sourced. Not a finding about NHS clients, and no statement about algorithmic bias.

Generated by `cap_sensitivity.py`. Rate 7.40% (Freddie Mac PMMS, 2026-10-08). Price proxy: 746 Compton single-family parcels, Assessor base-year values for 2025-26 resets, **not recorded sale prices**. 4-person caps. Front-end ratio 30% unless stated. 3% down, conventional, 700-719 credit band.

## 7. Direction of bias

| Omitted or assumed | Effect on 'a cap-level household cannot reach a recent sale' |
|---|---|
| Existing debts (none in ACS; front-end ratio only) | Understates the barrier |
| Unfinanceable fixers and cash-only sales inside the 25th percentile | Understates the barrier (a financeable entry price is higher) |
| Base-year price vs today's rates and prices | Depends on price change since reset; trend or restrict the window |
| Credit effect on the interest rate (not modeled) | Understates the barrier for lower scores |
| Wealth (unobserved) | Cash-barrier shares unknowable; only the cash gap is reported |
| Non-arm's-length resets in the sample | Noise, likely biased low on price; filter |
| Assistance structure (grant vs repayable second) | Dollars counted equally; repayable seconds with payments would raise required income |

Most omissions push toward understating the barrier, so the defensible reading of section 1 is a lower bound on the income barrier, not a point estimate.

## 8. What this does not show

- No count of Median renter households by income band (needs ACS tables); so no statement that a share of households is excluded.
- Recent buyers need not resemble local renters. Reach is a share of sales, not of people.
- The `Single` filter drops condos and townhomes, which raises the 25th percentile.
- One city, one household size (4 persons), front-end ratio only.
- Nothing here is confirmed until the held-out city run matches the headline.
