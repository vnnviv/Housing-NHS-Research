Generated by `assistance_sizing.py`. Rate 7.40% (Freddie Mac PMMS, 2026-10-08). Price benchmark: 746 Compton single-family parcels in the LA County Assessor roll whose land and improvement base years were both reset in 2025-26. Household savings assumed: $10,000 (**placeholder - not NHS client data**).

**Illustrative, not a finding about NHS clients.** The savings level, the closing-cost rate, and the program amounts are assumptions or dated lookups. Read these as a structure for the answer, with dollar figures that move when the inputs are replaced.

## 6. Credit

Total assistance needed, 3% down, by credit band. The assistance model can leave the first mortgage at any LTV, but the rate card in the repo prices only the 97-LTV case by band, so each band's rate is applied as a multiplier on the 700-719 rates at every LTV. That is an approximation.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Very low income | 25th | $358,432 | $358,432 | $358,432 | $358,432 |
| Very low income | Median | $444,593 | $444,593 | $444,593 | $444,593 |
| Area median income | 25th | $268,885 | $268,885 | $268,885 | $268,885 |
| Area median income | Median | $355,046 | $355,046 | $355,046 | $355,046 |
| Lower income (= moderate) | 25th | $178,075 | $178,075 | $178,075 | $178,075 |
| Lower income (= moderate) | Median | $264,236 | $264,236 | $264,236 | $264,236 |

**Credit does not change the assistance amount in this model.** Every cell is identical across bands because, in each case, the assistance needed brings the first mortgage to 80% of price or below, where mortgage insurance is zero. Credit only changes the monthly cost on the way there.

**What this does not show.** Only the mortgage-insurance channel is modeled. Lower credit scores also usually mean a higher interest rate, which would raise the assistance needed, and that is not in the model. So this table supports 'better credit alone does not close the gap' for the insurance channel only. It does not size the effect of credit on the rate. That needs a rate-by-credit-band source, ideally NHS's own lender quotes.

## 7. How much the price benchmark matters

The conclusion about the size of the gap depends on which price benchmark stands in for "something that exists in the market". Median home, 3% down, lower-income and area-median tiers.

| Price benchmark | Median price | Lower-income tier | Area-median tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assessor 2025-26 resets, as observed | $633,926 | $264,236 | $355,046 |
| Assessor +5% (stress: assumes reset values lag sale prices) | $665,623 | $300,922 | $391,732 |
| Assessor +10% (stress: assumes reset values lag sale prices) | $697,319 | $337,607 | $428,417 |
| Zillow ZHVI median from the September concept (flagged there as the wrong dependency; shown for comparison) | $624,000 | $252,748 | $343,558 |

**Sensitivity.** Each extra $1 of price adds $1.16 of required assistance, because a higher price also raises the property-tax share of the monthly budget and shrinks the loan the household can carry. A 5% error in the benchmark price ($31,696) is therefore a larger error in the assistance figure.

The Assessor benchmark is not a vendor estimate, but it is not a recorded sale price either. For parcels reassessed in 2025-26 it should sit close to the purchase price; the stress rows are there to show what happens if it does not, not to claim that it does. A recent-sales file (`--sales-csv`) would replace the stress rows with a measured comparison.

## 10. What this does not model

- **Structure of the assistance.** Dollars are counted the same whether they are a grant, a forgivable second, or a repayable deferred second. Structure decides what a program costs to run, not how many dollars a household needs.
- **Lending limits.** No combined loan-to-value cap is checked. A second lien large enough to close the income part may not be permitted under existing programs, which is a design question, not a modeling one.
- **Mortgage insurance** is banded by first-mortgage LTV from one rate card at 700-719 FICO; 80-85% LTV is priced at the 90-LTV rate.
- **Down-payment choice drops out of the total** once the income part is positive: assistance reduces the loan to the same size regardless of the starting down payment. Only the split between cash and income parts depends on it. FHA differs slightly through its financed upfront premium.
- **One geography, one household size.** Compton only; four-person income limits only; flat 1.1% property tax.
- **Closing costs** at 2.5% of price are the least grounded input.
